# Nugegoda Outlet

## Address and location
Pizza Hut Nugegoda, 112 Sample Road, Nugegoda (sample address). Approximate coordinates: 6.8649, 79.8997. Close to the Nugegoda flyover and bus stand; public transport is easy.

## Opening hours
Nugegoda outlet is open every day from 10:30 to 23:00. Last dine-in orders at 22:15. Last delivery orders at 22:30.

## Services
Dine-in (about 45 seats), takeaway, and home delivery. Popular with families and students; Student Tuesday offers are honoured here.

## Delivery area
Nugegoda standard delivery radius is 8 km. Areas commonly served: Nugegoda, Delkanda, Maharagama, Gangodawila, Dehiwala, Rajagiriya, and Kotte. Zone 1 (0 to 3 km) free delivery; Zone 2 (3 to 8 km) has a fee quoted at checkout.

## Facilities
Air-conditioned dining area, high chairs, ground-floor access, free Wi-Fi. Limited motorcycle parking in front; car parking on side streets.

## Manager
Nugegoda Branch Manager: Kasun Fernando.
