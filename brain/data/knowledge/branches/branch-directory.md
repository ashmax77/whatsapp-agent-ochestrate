# Branch Directory

## All outlets at a glance
We have three outlets (sample data):
- **Colombo 07 (Cinnamon Gardens):** dine-in, takeaway, delivery. Open daily 10:30 to 23:00.
- **Nugegoda:** dine-in, takeaway, delivery. Open daily 10:30 to 23:00.
- **Kandy (City Centre):** dine-in, takeaway, delivery. Open daily 11:00 to 22:30.

## Which branch serves my area?
Delivery is handled by the nearest outlet. Customers in Colombo 01 to 08 are usually served by Colombo 07. Customers in Nugegoda, Maharagama, Dehiwala, Mount Lavinia, Rajagiriya, and Kotte are usually served by Nugegoda. Customers in Kandy city, Peradeniya, and Katugastota are served by Kandy. Share your location pin or area name and the assistant will check your delivery zone.
