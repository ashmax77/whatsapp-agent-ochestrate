# Kandy Outlet

## Address and location
Pizza Hut Kandy outlet is located at 8 Sample Street, Kandy City Centre (sample address) (Kandy branch எங்கே இருக்கு / கண்டி கிளை எங்கே உள்ளது / නුවර ශාඛාව කොහෙද පිහිටා තිබෙන්නේ). Approximate coordinates: 7.2906, 80.6337. Within walking distance of Kandy Lake and the city bus stand.

## Opening hours
Kandy outlet is open every day from 11:00 to 22:30. Last dine-in orders at 21:45. Last delivery orders at 22:00.

## Services
Dine-in (about 50 seats), takeaway, and home delivery. Tourist-friendly; menus are available in English.

## Delivery area
Kandy standard delivery radius is 6 km because of hilly roads. Areas served: Kandy city, Peradeniya, Katugastota, Suduhumpola, and Asgiriya. Delivery times may be longer in heavy rain or during the Esala Perahera season due to road closures.

## Facilities
Air-conditioned dining, high chairs, step-free entrance, free Wi-Fi, small outdoor seating area.
