# Colombo 07 Outlet

## Address and location
Pizza Hut Colombo 07, 25 Sample Avenue, Cinnamon Gardens, Colombo 07 (sample address). Approximate coordinates: 6.9060, 79.8650. Street parking is limited; a paid car park is available nearby.

## Opening hours
Colombo 07 outlet is open every day from 10:30 to 23:00. Last dine-in orders at 22:15. Last delivery orders at 22:30.

## Services
Dine-in (about 60 seats), takeaway, and home delivery. Birthday table bookings for groups of 6 or more can be made by phone at least one day ahead.

## Delivery area
Colombo 07 standard delivery radius is 8 km. Zone 1 (0 to 3 km) has free delivery; Zone 2 (3 to 8 km) has a delivery fee quoted at checkout. Areas beyond 8 km cannot be accepted.

## Facilities
Air-conditioned dining area, kids' high chairs, wheelchair-accessible entrance and restroom, free Wi-Fi.

Colombo 07 Branch Manager: Nimali Perera. Customers may ask for the manager by role.
