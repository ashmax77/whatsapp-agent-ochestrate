# Leave and Attendance Policy (sample)

## Attendance
Arrive 10 minutes before the shift. Repeated lateness is reviewed with the Branch Manager.

## Annual leave
Employees receive annual leave according to Sri Lankan labour law and company policy. Submit requests at least one week ahead (two weeks for peak days).

## Sick leave
Inform the Branch Manager before the shift when possible. Staff with symptoms of illness must not handle food. A medical certificate is required for absences of 2 or more consecutive days.

## Public holidays
Staff who work on public holidays receive time off in lieu or the compensation set out in their contract.

## Uniform and equipment
Uniforms are provided; damaged or lost equipment must be reported the same day.
