# WhatsApp Bot Escalation Rules (staff guide)

## When the bot hands over to staff
The bot hands over when a customer makes a complaint; asks for a refund or compensation; mentions a severe allergy or illness after eating; is abusive or very frustrated; asks for the manager; or when the bot fails three times in a row.

## What staff receive
A message with the customer's ID, the reason, the last five messages, and the current cart (if any). The customer is told that the team will reply shortly.

## Staff commands (staff numbers only)
- `/resume <user_id>`: return the customer to the bot.
- `/status <order_no> <status>`: update an order (accepted, preparing, out_for_delivery, ready_for_pickup, completed, cancelled).
- `/orders`: list recent new orders.

## Response targets
Respond to handoffs within 30 minutes in opening hours. Food-safety and allergy cases are answered immediately by the Branch Manager.

## After resolving
Record the outcome in the order dashboard, then resume the bot. Share recurring issues with the Customer Care Supervisor.
