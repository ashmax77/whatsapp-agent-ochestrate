# Employee Code of Conduct (sample)

## Respect and professionalism
Treat colleagues and customers with courtesy. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and abusive language are not tolerated.

## Appearance and hygiene
Wear the clean uniform, hairnet, and gloves in food areas; wash hands frequently; no jewellery in the kitchen apart from a plain wedding ring.

## Customer data
Never share customer phone numbers, addresses, or order history outside the job. Do not take photos of customers' chats or orders.

## Cash handling
Count cash with a colleague at shift end. Report shortages immediately.

## Social media
Do not post kitchen photos, customer information, or complaints on social media without approval.

## Conflicts of interest
Do not accept gifts or payments from suppliers or customers for special treatment.

## Reporting concerns
Report safety, ethical, or harassment concerns to the Branch Manager or the Area Manager. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is prohibited.
