# Shift Patterns (sample)

## Standard shifts
Morning (prep) shift runs 08:30 to 16:30: dough, sauces, ingredient prep, opening checks. Evening shift runs 14:30 to 23:30: peak service, closing, and cleaning. Staff work 6 days with 1 weekly day off, rotated by the Branch Manager.

## Peak staffing
Friday, Saturday, and public holidays require full staffing; leave requests for these days need two weeks' notice.

## Minimum staffing per outlet at peak
1 manager or shift lead, 2 cooks, 1 cashier, and 2 riders.

## Handoff coverage
Between 10:30 and 23:00 one staff member per outlet monitors the WhatsApp handoff queue. Outside these hours the bot tells customers that staff will respond the next morning.
