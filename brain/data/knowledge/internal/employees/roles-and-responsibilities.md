# Roles and Responsibilities

## Area Manager
Oversees all outlets, handles serious escalations (food safety, injuries, repeated complaints), approves promotions, and reviews monthly performance.

## Branch Manager
Runs one outlet: staffing, stock, quality checks, complaint decisions (replacements, credit notes), and cash reconciliation. First human contact for customer handoffs from the bot.

## Shift Lead
Supervises each shift, assigns tasks, checks order accuracy before dispatch, handles minor customer issues, and updates order status in the system.

## Head Cook
Controls kitchen quality, portion standards, ingredient freshness, and hygiene. Has the final say on whether a customised order is feasible.

## Cashier / Order Desk
Takes dine-in and phone orders, processes payments, prints receipts, and monitors incoming WhatsApp orders from the order dashboard.

## Delivery Rider
Delivers orders safely and politely, keeps insulated bags clean, collects payment, and reports address problems immediately. Does not handle refunds.

## Customer Care Supervisor
Monitors the WhatsApp assistant, takes over handoff conversations, reviews flagged chats, and reports recurring problems to the Area Manager.
