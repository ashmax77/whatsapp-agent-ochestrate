# Cancellation Policy

## Before baking
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they go into the oven, usually within 5 minutes of confirmation.

## After baking has started
Once preparation has started, orders cannot be cancelled. Please contact the outlet; the manager may decide case by case.

## Out for delivery
Orders already dispatched cannot be cancelled. If the customer refuses delivery without a valid reason, we may decline future cash-on-delivery orders from the same number.

## How to cancel
Tell the WhatsApp assistant immediately, or call the outlet. The assistant will check whether cancellation is still possible and connect you to staff if needed.
