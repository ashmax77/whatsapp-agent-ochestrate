# Privacy Policy (summary)

## What we collect
We collect your WhatsApp number, the name shown on WhatsApp, delivery address, location pin if shared, and order history so we can process and deliver your orders.

## How we use your data
Only to prepare and deliver orders, contact you about your order, provide support, and (if you join) manage loyalty points. We do not sell your data.

## Voice notes
If you send a voice note, we convert it to text to understand your order. The audio is deleted after processing.

## Retention
Order records are kept for 12 months. Chat logs are kept for 30 days. You may ask us to delete your data by contacting staff.

## Security
Access to customer data is limited to authorised staff and systems. Payment card details are never collected through WhatsApp.

## Asking for your data
To see or delete your data, ask the assistant to connect you to staff or call the outlet.
