# Payment and Refunds

## Accepted payment methods
All payments, prices, and bills are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). We accept cash on delivery and card on delivery (the rider carries a card machine). Online card payment and bank transfers are not available through WhatsApp.

## Receipts
A printed receipt is given with the order. The WhatsApp assistant sends a text summary after confirmation.

## Wrong or missing items
If an item is missing or incorrect, tell us within 30 minutes of delivery, with a photo if possible. We will deliver the correct item or issue a credit note.

## Quality complaints
If the food arrives cold, burnt, or not as described, contact us within 30 minutes. A replacement or credit note is provided after review by the branch manager.

## Refunds
Cash refunds are not given for completed deliveries. Credit notes valid for 60 days are issued instead. Refund and credit decisions are made by staff, never by the assistant.

## Customer errors
We cannot refund orders delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer or when the customer is unreachable.
