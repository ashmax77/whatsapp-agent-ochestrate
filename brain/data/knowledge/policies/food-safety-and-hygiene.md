# Food Safety and Hygiene

## Kitchen standards
All outlets follow documented food-safety procedures: staff wash hands before handling food, wear gloves and hairnets, and use colour-coded chopping boards for raw and cooked items.

## Ingredient handling
Ingredients are stored at safe temperatures and checked against expiry dates daily. Chicken is cooked to a safe core temperature before use.

## Delivery hygiene
Riders use insulated, sanitised delivery bags. Drinks and desserts are sealed.

## Illness policy
Staff with symptoms of illness must report to the manager and do not handle food until cleared.

## Allergies
We cannot guarantee allergen-free preparation. Severe allergies must be discussed with staff.

## Reporting a food safety concern
If you believe you received unsafe food, keep the item and packaging, stop eating, and contact the outlet immediately. This is escalated to the branch manager the same day.
