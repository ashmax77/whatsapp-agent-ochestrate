# Ordering and Delivery Policy

## Minimum order
The minimum food subtotal for delivery orders is LKR 1,500. Pickup orders have no minimum.

## Delivery fee and zones
Zone 1 (0 to 3 km) is free. Zone 2 (3 to 8 km) has a fee shown before you confirm. The Kandy outlet's maximum radius is 6 km. Orders outside the radius cannot be accepted.

## Delivery time
Estimated 35 to 45 minutes, up to 60 minutes at peak times. The estimate is not a guarantee. If the delay exceeds 75 minutes, contact us and we will check the order.

## Address requirements
Please provide a full address with a landmark, or share a WhatsApp location pin. Our rider may call to confirm the location, so keep your phone available.

## Order confirmation
An order is placed only after the customer confirms the summary with a clear "yes". Orders are never placed by guessing or assuming.

## Changing an order
Changes are possible before the order goes into the oven (usually within 5 minutes). After that, contact the outlet by phone.

## Large orders
Orders of more than 10 pizzas or catering requests need at least 24 hours' notice and must be arranged with the outlet manager.

## Delivery to offices, hotels, and apartments
Please include the building name, floor, and a contact number. Riders cannot enter secured areas and may ask you to meet them at the gate or reception.
