# Complaint Handling

## How to complain
Tell the WhatsApp assistant or call the outlet. The assistant politely acknowledges the issue, collects the order details, and hands the case to staff.

## What the assistant does and does not do
The assistant does not argue, promise refunds, or decide compensation. It collects the order number, the problem, and a photo if available, then connects the customer to a staff member.

## Response time
Staff aim to respond within 30 minutes during opening hours. Complaints made after hours are answered the next morning by 11:00.

## Escalation
Complaints about food safety, allergic reactions, rider misconduct, or repeated issues are escalated to the branch manager and the area manager.

## Possible outcomes
A replacement item, a credit note, an apology, or an explanation. The final decision rests with the branch manager.
