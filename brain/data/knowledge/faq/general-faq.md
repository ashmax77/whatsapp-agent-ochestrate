# General FAQ

## Do you deliver?
Yes, within 8 km of the Colombo 07 and Nugegoda outlets and 6 km of the Kandy outlet. Share your location pin or area name and the assistant will check.

## How long does delivery take?
Usually 35 to 45 minutes; up to 60 minutes at peak times.

## Can I pay online?
Not through WhatsApp. Pay by cash or card on delivery or at pickup.

## Do you have vegetarian options?
Yes: Margherita, Veggie Delight, Cheese Lover, and several sides and desserts.

## Is anything vegan or gluten-free?
We do not have a dedicated vegan pizza and gluten-free bases are not available.

## Can I order by voice note?
Yes. The assistant will repeat back what it understood so you can confirm.

## Can I call the WhatsApp number?
The WhatsApp number is for messages and voice notes only; calls are declined automatically. Please call the outlet number for phone orders.

## Can I change or cancel my order?
Within about 5 minutes, before it goes into the oven. After that, contact the outlet.

## Do you offer discounts?
See the current offers: Friday Feast, Student Tuesday, Family Combo, and free Zone 1 delivery.

## Can I book a table?
Yes, by phone. Groups of 6 or more should book at least one day ahead.
