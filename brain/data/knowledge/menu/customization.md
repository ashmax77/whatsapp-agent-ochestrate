# Sizes, Crusts and Customization

## Pizza sizes
Personal is about 7 inches and suits one person. Medium is about 9 inches and suits 1 to 2 people. Large is about 12 inches and suits 2 to 3 people. Sides come in a single regular portion unless stated.

## Crust options
Classic Pan is soft and fluffy with a golden edge (default). Thin and Crispy is a lighter, crunchier base. Cheese-Stuffed Edge has mozzarella baked into the crust; an extra charge applies and the ordering system shows it. Crust availability may vary by outlet.

## Extra toppings
Customers can add extra cheese, mushrooms, olives, onions, capsicum, sweetcorn, or chicken to any pizza. Each extra topping carries an additional charge shown at checkout.

## Spice adjustments
Customers can request "less spicy" or "extra spicy" on pizzas such as Devilled Chicken or Chicken Tikka Masala. These requests are recorded as order notes.

## Half-and-half pizzas
Half-and-half pizzas are not available in this sample menu. Customers can order two personal pizzas instead.

## Notes to the kitchen
Customers may add short notes such as "no onions" or "well done". Notes about allergies are handled by staff; see the allergen information.
