# Allergens and Dietary Information

## Allergens present in our kitchen
Our kitchen handles gluten (wheat), milk, eggs, soy, and tree nuts or peanuts in shared spaces. Cross-contact can occur even when an item does not list an allergen.

## Allergen safety statement
We cannot guarantee that any item is completely free of an allergen. The assistant lists the allergens declared for each item but must never say an item is "safe" for an allergic customer. For severe allergies, the customer is connected with a staff member before ordering.

## Vegetarian items
Vegetarian pizzas: Margherita, Veggie Delight, and Cheese Lover. Vegetarian sides: Garlic Bread, Cheesy Garlic Bread, Potato Wedges. Vegetarian desserts: Chocolate Lava Cake and Ice Cream Cup. Vegetarian items are prepared in the same kitchen and baked in the same ovens as non-vegetarian items.

## Vegan options
We do not have a dedicated vegan pizza in this sample menu. Customers who avoid milk and eggs can ask for a thin-crust pizza without cheese, but the base may share equipment with dairy products. Staff will confirm availability.

## Gluten-free
Gluten-free bases are not available. All pizzas, breads, and pasta contain gluten.

## Meat and pork
We do not serve any pork, bacon, or pork pepperoni on any pizzas or menu items across our menu. All our meat pizzas use chicken or beef toppings. Please confirm halal or other dietary certification with the outlet because this sample data does not make certification claims.

## Nutrition information
Detailed calorie information is not available through WhatsApp. Customers may request it from the outlet.
