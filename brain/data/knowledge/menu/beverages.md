# Beverages

## Soft drinks
Bottled soft drinks (500 ml) in cola, lemon-lime, and orange flavours. Larger 1.5 litre bottles are available for family orders. Vegetarian: yes. No listed allergens.

## Iced lime juice
Freshly made iced lime juice, lightly sweetened. Available at dine-in and takeaway; delivery orders receive it in a sealed cup. Vegan: yes.

## Mineral water
Still bottled water, 500 ml. Vegan: yes.

## Hot beverages (dine-in only)
Tea and coffee are served at dine-in only and are not part of the delivery menu. Contains milk unless ordered without.
