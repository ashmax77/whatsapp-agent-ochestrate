# Pizzas and Pricing

## Currency
All prices across our menu and orders are charged in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). All pizzas are available in Personal, Medium, and Large sizes.

## Margherita
Margherita (`pz_margherita`) is a classic vegetarian pizza with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil on a hand-stretched base. Mild flavour, a good choice for children and first-time customers. Vegetarian: yes. Contains: gluten, milk. Spice level: none. Available: yes.
- Personal: LKR 1,490
- Medium: LKR 2,490
- Large: LKR 3,490

## Supreme
Supreme (`pz_supreme`) is our loaded favourite: pepperoni-style chicken sausage, chicken ham, mushrooms, capsicum, and onions with mozzarella. Savoury and filling, best shared. Vegetarian: no. Contains: gluten, milk. Spice level: mild. Available: yes.
- Personal: LKR 1,790
- Medium: LKR 2,890
- Large: LKR 3,990

## Veggie Delight
Veggie Delight (`pz_veggie`) is a colourful vegetarian pizza with mushrooms, sweet corn, black olives, capsicum, onions, and tomato on a cheese base. Vegetarian: yes. Contains: gluten, milk. Spice level: mild. Available: yes.
- Personal: LKR 1,590
- Medium: LKR 2,590
- Large: LKR 3,590

## Garlic Bread
Garlic Bread (`sd_garlic_bread`) is toasted bread with garlic butter and herbs, served hot. One regular portion. Vegetarian: yes. Contains: gluten, milk. Available: yes.
- Regular: LKR 690

## Chicken Tikka Masala
Chicken Tikka Masala pizza has chicken marinated in tikka spices with onions, capsicum, and a drizzle of mint mayo over mozzarella. Warm, aromatic, and medium-spicy. Vegetarian: no. Contains: gluten, milk, egg (mayo). Spice level: medium.

## BBQ Chicken
BBQ Chicken pizza has grilled chicken pieces, red onion, and sweetcorn with smoky barbecue sauce and mozzarella. Sweet and smoky with no heat. Vegetarian: no. Contains: gluten, milk, soy. Spice level: mild.

## Devilled Chicken (Sri Lankan special)
Devilled Chicken is our local favourite: chicken in a sweet, tangy, and spicy devilled sauce with capsicum, onions, and spring onion over mozzarella. Bold flavour and a noticeable kick. Vegetarian: no. Contains: gluten, milk, soy. Spice level: hot. Customers who prefer less heat can ask for "less spicy" in the order notes.

## Cheese Lover
Cheese Lover is a four-cheese pizza with mozzarella, cheddar, processed cheese, and a light herb seasoning. Rich and creamy. Vegetarian: yes. Contains: gluten, milk. Spice level: none.
