# Sides and Desserts
Exact prices come from the ordering system, not this file.

## Garlic Bread
Garlic Bread (`sd_garlic_bread`) is toasted bread with garlic butter and herbs, served hot. One regular portion (4 pieces). Vegetarian: yes. Contains: gluten, milk. Available: yes.
- Price: LKR 690

## Cheesy Garlic Bread
Cheesy Garlic Bread is the garlic bread topped with melted mozzarella. One regular portion (4 pieces). Vegetarian: yes. Contains: gluten, milk.

## Chicken Wings
Chicken Wings come as six crispy wings tossed in your choice of sauce: BBQ (mild), Hot Devilled (hot), or Plain Salted (no spice). Vegetarian: no. Contains: gluten, soy (sauces).

## Potato Wedges
Potato Wedges are seasoned wedges served with a tomato dip. Vegetarian: yes. Fried in a shared fryer, so traces of gluten are possible.

## Chicken Alfredo Pasta
Chicken Alfredo Pasta is penne in a creamy white sauce with grilled chicken and mushrooms, finished with parmesan. Vegetarian: no. Contains: gluten, milk, egg.

## Chocolate Lava Cake
Chocolate Lava Cake is a warm chocolate cake with a molten centre. It takes about 10 minutes to bake, so it may arrive slightly after the pizza when dining in. Vegetarian: yes. Contains: gluten, milk, egg.

## Ice Cream Cup
Ice Cream Cup is a single cup of vanilla or chocolate ice cream. Vegetarian: yes. Contains: milk. Not available for delivery beyond Zone 1 because it may melt.
