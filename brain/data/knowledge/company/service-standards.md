# Service Standards

## Delivery time commitment
We aim to deliver within 35 to 45 minutes for addresses inside our standard delivery area. During peak hours (Friday and Saturday evenings, public holidays, and rainy-day rushes) delivery can take up to 60 minutes. The assistant will quote an estimated time when you place an order.

## Food temperature and quality
Pizzas leave the oven hot and are packed in insulated delivery bags. If your food arrives cold, damaged, or incorrect, contact us immediately so we can resolve it. See the Payment and Refunds policy.

## Order accuracy
Staff check every order against the receipt before dispatch. The assistant always reads your order back and asks for a clear "yes" before the order is sent to the kitchen.

## Courtesy
Our team is trained to be polite and helpful. If you experience rude behaviour, please report it through the complaint process.

## Accessibility
All sample outlets have step-free entrances. Please tell staff about any accessibility needs when you arrive or when calling ahead.
