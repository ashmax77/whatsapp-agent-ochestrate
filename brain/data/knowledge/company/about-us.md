# About Us

## Who we are
Pizza Hut (sample outlet group) is a casual dining and delivery restaurant brand serving freshly baked pizzas, pasta, sides, and desserts. This sample group runs three outlets in Sri Lanka: Colombo 07, Nugegoda, and Kandy. We serve dine-in, takeaway (pickup), and home delivery customers.

## What we are known for
Hand-stretched dough baked fresh to order, a generous cheese blend, and a menu that mixes classic pizzas with Sri Lankan-inspired flavours such as the Devilled Chicken pizza. Every pizza is prepared when ordered; we do not use pre-baked bases.

## Our promise
Hot food, accurate orders, and polite service. If something is wrong with an order, we fix it or make it right. See the complaint handling and refund policies for details.

## Ordering channels
Customers can order at the outlet, by phone, and through this WhatsApp assistant. Online card payment is not available through WhatsApp; payment is made on delivery or at pickup.

## Languages we support
The WhatsApp assistant can chat in English, Sinhala, and Tamil, including mixed-language messages. For complex issues it will connect you to a staff member.
