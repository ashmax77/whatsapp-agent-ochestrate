# Current Offers (as of 9 November 2026)
The ordering system (promos.json) is the source of truth for rules. Only one offer can be used per order unless stated.

## Friday Feast (code FRIDAY10)
Friday Feast gives 10% off the food subtotal every Friday for orders with a food subtotal of LKR 3,000 or more. Valid for delivery and pickup. Cannot be combined with other offers. The delivery fee is not discounted. Valid until further notice.

## Student Tuesday (code STUDENT15)
Student Tuesday gives 15% off on Tuesdays when a valid student ID is shown at pickup or on delivery. Minimum food subtotal LKR 2,000. Valid at the Nugegoda and Kandy outlets only. Valid until 31 December 2026.

## Family Combo (no code needed)
Family Combo is a large pizza, a medium pizza (both from the standard menu), garlic bread, and a 1.5 litre soft drink at a bundled price shown in the ordering system. Available daily at all outlets. Substitutions are not allowed except choosing different pizzas.

## Free delivery in Zone 1
Orders delivered within 0 to 3 km of the outlet have free delivery every day, subject to the standard minimum order value.

## Upcoming: Christmas Season Offer
A Christmas season offer is planned for 15 to 31 December 2026. Details will be announced. The assistant must not promise specific benefits until it is listed under Current Offers.

## How offers are applied
The customer tells the assistant the code, or the assistant suggests an eligible offer. The ordering system checks the day, minimum spend, and outlet before applying any discount. The assistant must not invent or guess offers.
