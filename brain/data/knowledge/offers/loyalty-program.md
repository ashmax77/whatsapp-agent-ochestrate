# Loyalty Program (Hut Rewards, sample)

## How it works
Customers earn 1 point for every LKR 100 spent on food (delivery fees excluded). Points are linked to the customer's WhatsApp number once they place an order.

## Redeeming points
100 points can be redeemed for a free Garlic Bread; 300 points for a free Medium pizza (customer's choice, up to Supreme or Chicken Tikka Masala). Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Redemption is done by staff at confirmation; the assistant explains the program but does not apply redemptions in this version.

## Birthday treat
Members receive a free Chocolate Lava Cake with any pizza order in their birthday week. Staff verify the birthday on delivery or at pickup.

## Fair use
Points have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and may be removed in case of misuse.
